How the private Gmail metadata integration handles Google account data.

In brief: this connection uses Gmail metadata to track selected business conversations. It does not read email bodies or attachments and has no permission to send, draft, or modify email.

1. Scope

Ryan Roncal operates GenesisVA Lead Operations as a private business lead and reply-tracking tool. This policy covers its GenesisVA Gmail metadata integration, the associated private outreach queue, and this public information site. The Google Cloud project may also contain other OAuth clients; this policy does not describe unrelated integrations or their permissions.

The integration is configured for the owner’s account. It is not a public email service. It is intended for business contact information; do not submit patient records or clinical information.

2. Google data access

The Gmail connection requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata. That permission technically allows mailbox-wide metadata access. The configured workflow narrows its use to the mailbox profile and specific messages or threads explicitly linked to records in the private outreach queue.

For those checks, the workflow may access:

  • The connected mailbox’s email address.
  • Gmail message and thread identifiers, labels, and timestamps.
  • Selected headers: From, To, Cc, Bcc, Subject, Date, Message-ID, In-Reply-To, References, and Auto-Submitted.

It does not request access to message bodies or attachments. It does not request permission to send messages, create Gmail drafts, or modify mailbox contents.

3. Purpose and limits

Google data is used to confirm the connected mailbox, validate a manually linked sent message, recognize an incoming response in the linked conversation, and update reply or follow-up review statuses. An incoming response pauses the sequence for owner review. The connection does not automatically send outreach.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. The owner reviews the tracking results. Any service-provider human access to Google user data is limited by the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

GenesisVA Lead Operations’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and sharing

The configured data flow uses these systems:

  • Self-hosted n8n on the owner’s computer: stores the OAuth connection in its credential store and processes the returned metadata. Execution history may retain metadata payloads until that history is pruned or manually deleted.
  • Base44: stores private business lead records, the outreach queue, mailbox connection status, message or thread identifiers, and derived sent, reply, and follow-up timestamps or statuses. Google OAuth tokens are not transferred to Base44 by this integration.
  • Google Sheets: receives the business lead and outreach status projection, such as recipient, readiness, reply status, and relevant timestamps.
  • Google: provides Gmail authorization and API access and hosts the connected Sheet.

Data is handled by these service providers as needed to operate the tool. It is not shared with unrelated recipients. The private application uses administrator access controls; access to the owner’s computer, n8n instance, Google account, and Sheet must also remain restricted.

5. Retention

The owner retains connection data and outreach records for as long as needed for the tracking workflow. Application status records do not currently have an automatic expiry and are removed manually when no longer needed. n8n execution history follows the local instance’s configured pruning settings or manual deletion.

Disconnecting Google does not automatically erase previously stored statuses, identifiers, Sheet entries, or execution histories. Provider backups may remain subject to the relevant provider’s retention process. This policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period.

6. Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the connection through Google Account third-party connections and remove its n8n credential. Revoking Google access stops future authorized reads once existing access is invalidated; stored copies are handled separately.

For access, correction, or deletion of information associated with this integration, contact ryanjroncal@gmail.com. Deletion must cover the relevant Base44 records, Google Sheet copies, and local n8n execution histories where applicable. The operator may need to confirm a requestor’s authority before changing records.

7. This public information site

This static site contains no login, lead intake form, advertising, analytics script, or application data. It does not connect to Gmail. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the pages. Following an external link or opening an email client is subject to that service’s own privacy practices.

8. Contact and changes

Contact Ryan Roncal at ryanjroncal@gmail.com for questions about this integration or policy. Material changes to Google data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Additional Google permissions would require a separate authorization.
