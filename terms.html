1. Purpose and access

GenesisVA Lead Operations is Ryan Roncal’s private tool for organizing business leads, preparing internal outreach drafts, and reviewing metadata-based reply and follow-up statuses. Access is limited to the authorized owner. Publishing these information pages does not grant access to the private application.

2. Owner responsibility

The owner is responsible for account security, appropriate access to the connected systems, checking business contact information, honoring opt-outs, and reviewing any communication before sending it separately. Tracking statuses are aids to review and may be incomplete or stale.

The tool is intended for business contact information. It must not be used to collect patient records or clinical information. The current workflow keeps sending disabled; an internal draft or follow-up status is not permission to contact a person.

3. Google connection

The GenesisVA integration requests Gmail metadata permission only. It cannot use that permission to read message bodies or attachments, create Gmail drafts, send messages, or modify email. The privacy policy explains the configured use, storage, retention, revocation, and deletion of Google data.

The owner may disconnect the integration through Google Account settings. Google account access remains subject to Google’s applicable terms. Other OAuth clients in the same Cloud project may have separate purposes and permissions.

4. Availability and review

The tool depends on the owner’s self-hosted n8n instance, Base44, Google services, and a working internet connection. Service interruptions, expired authorizations, or configuration changes can interrupt tracking. The owner should review current records and the actual conversation in Gmail before acting on a status.

5. Scope of these terms

These terms describe the private operations integration. They do not offer access to the tool to the public, create a virtual-assistant service purchase, or replace a separate client service agreement. This information site does not collect quote requests or process payments.

6. Contact and changes

Questions can be sent to Ryan Roncal at ryanjroncal@gmail.com. Changes to the integration or these terms will be reflected on this site with an updated effective date.
